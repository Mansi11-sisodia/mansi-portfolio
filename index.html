my approach

How I Work

01
🔍

Understand the problem

I sit with a problem before jumping to solutions — asking what's really going on and who it affects.

02
🗣️

Listen to people

Good decisions start with talking to the right people. I'm curious about what people actually do, not just what they say they do.

03
🗂️

Organise the moving parts

Chaos is fine at the start, but someone has to hold the threads. I like keeping things coordinated and tracked.

04
⚡

Turn ideas into action

At some point the plan has to become a stall, a prototype or a real conversation. I'd rather do than plan.

05
🔄

Test, learn and improve

The first version is never perfect — that's fine. Try it, see what happens and do better next time.
